Small parts people forget — these are the ones most likely to be your missing order:
  • Bulk cap 1000 µF — easy to overlook, but the 5 V rail wants it
  • 1 kΩ base resistors ×4 — the 2N2222 LED drivers don't work without them
  • Hookup wire — especially the heavier 20 AWG for the 12 V feed
  • The 3 A fuse itself (if your holder didn't include the right value)

12 V Front-End — Perfboard Layout

30 × 70 mm perfboard, 24 columns × 10 rows of usable holes at standard 0.1″ (2.54 mm) pitch. No bus rails — every connection below is a direct wire between two real points, routed left-to-right so nothing backtracks. Coordinates are real hole addresses: column, row — column 1 at the left, row 1 at the top, row 10 is the physical bottom edge.

Board edges: 12 V input enters the left edge. The two bucks sit off-board to the right — three flying leads each (12V in, shared GND, voltage-return), not four — MP1584 modules are non-isolated, so input and output GND are the same net inside the module. The two JST-XH connectors mount right-angle along the bottom edge (row 10), wires exiting downward to the base deck.
Not yet verified: JST-XH pitch is the standard 2.5 mm (matches your perfboard grid closely enough to use as-is). I have not checked your specific right-angle parts' shroud width or edge overhang — dry-fit both connectors against the board before soldering anything near row 10.

How to read it

No rails. Every colored line is one specific wire between two named points — nothing is a bus you tap into later. Follow the chain in the order it's drawn (left to right) and you won't backtrack across the board.

+12V protected chain: Zener-top → Source → TVS-top → 3V Buck IN+ → 5V Buck IN+.

GND chain: GND-in → resistor bottom → TVS bottom → connector GND pin 1 → pin 2 → 3V Buck GND → 5V Buck GND.

Buck return signals (3.5V, 5V) are the only two nets that aren't part of a chain — each is a single flying lead from its buck straight down to its own connector pin.

Build order

  1. Drop in the IRF9540N (row 5, columns 10–12), then Zener and 100 kΩ around it, then TVS at column 16. Check stripe direction on both diodes.
  2. Land the fused +12V-in wire on the Drain pad and the GND-in wire at col 2, row 9.
  3. Run the +12V chain: Zener-top → Source → TVS-top → 3V Buck IN+ → 5V Buck IN+.
  4. Run the GND chain: GND-in → resistor bottom → TVS bottom → connector GND pins → both buck GNDs.
  5. Dry-fit both JST-XH right-angle connectors against row 10 before soldering — confirm the housings don't overlap or hang off the board edge somewhere unexpected.
  6. Solder the 8-pin data and 4-pin power connectors, then the two return jumpers (3.5V and 5V, columns 21/22, row 6 down to row 10).
  7. Buzz every wire with a meter before connecting anything downstream — confirm no short between +12V and GND, and that the return jumpers didn't pick up a short passing near the GND wire.

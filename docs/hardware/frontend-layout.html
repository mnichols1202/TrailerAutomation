12 V Front-End — Perfboard Layout

Where each part physically sits so the wiring stays clean. The trick: two horizontal rails (+12 V on top, GND on the bottom) with the small parts standing vertically between them. No crossing wires.

+12 V (orange) GND (dark) Gate (blue) diode stripe = cathode

How to read it

Top orange line = your +12 V-out rail. Everything that needs protected +12 V lands on it: the transistor's Source, the top of the Zener, the top of the TVS, and the wires out to both bucks' IN+.

Bottom dark line = your ground rail. Everything ground lands on it: the bottom of the 100k, the bottom of the TVS, the GND wire from the XT30, and both bucks' IN−.

The transistor sits in the middle. Fused +12 V drops into the Drain from the top; the Source jumps up to the +12 V rail. That's the only spot power passes through a part.

Each rail is just a bare wire soldered along one row of holes on the back of the board. Every part reaches up or down to a rail — nothing has to cross anything.

Build order

  1. Solder a bare wire along the top row = +12 V rail. Solder another along the bottom row = GND rail.
  2. Drop the IRF9540N in the middle, legs down (G–D–S).
  3. Source → up to the +12 V rail (short jumper).
  4. 100k from the Gate down to the GND rail.
  5. Zener from the Gate up to the +12 V rail — stripe end to the +12 V rail.
  6. TVS from the +12 V rail down to the GND rail — stripe end to the +12 V rail.
  7. Bring your fused +12 V wire to the Drain, and your XT30 ground wire to the GND rail.
  8. Run two wires off the +12 V rail to the bucks' IN+, and two off the GND rail to the bucks' IN−.
